Is the 1994 cassette sampler Neticated available as mp3 online?

No, the 1994 cassette sampler Neticated—a compilation put together by participants of the rec.music.gdead Usenet newsgroup featuring Grateful Dead cover bands and related projects (including members associated with Brainstatik)—is not widely available as an official MP3 release online. Because it was an independently distributed cassette and DAT tape tree project shared among newsgroup members at the time, digital rips of the release are generally not hosted on standard commercial streaming or MP3 platforms.
