About Me

I am a reliable drummer with over 40 years of playing experience. My core roots span rock and progressive music, alongside a passionate focus on jazz and odd time signatures.

I am currently looking for low-commitment musical connections—ideally small jazz combos (duos, trios, quartets) focused on improvising over classic jazz standards (such as those from The Real Book), as well as casual home jams or community ensembles with no high-pressure demands.

My playing style embraces odd time signatures, time changes, polyrhythms, and the expansive sound palette offered by electronic drums.

  • Jazz Experience: Performed in academic ensembles, the Bell Labs Holmdel Big Band, and numerous jam sessions.
  • Rock & Blues Experience: Former member of bands including Solar Circus, The Callipygian Blues Band, Gangster Clairvoyants, and Brainstatik.
  • Solid timekeeper who respects stage volume.
  • Equipped with both acoustic and electronic drum kits.
  • Dynamic Sensitivity & Restraint: Deeply aware that music is as much about space as it is about sound. I focus on nuanced dynamics to give the music room to breathe.
  • Reliable, easy-going, good ears, and drama-free.

What I’m Looking For

  • Small Jazz Combos (Ideal): Joining or forming a small ensemble (trio, quartet, or quintet) to explore casual improvisation on classic jazz standards.
  • Casual Jams: Meeting up with local guitarists, pianists, bassists, or saxophonists to work through jazz standards and casual improvisation.
  • Rehearsal Ensembles: Community big bands or casual groups meeting weekly or bi-weekly purely for fun.
  • Sub Work: Filling in for local jazz gigs when your regular drummer is unavailable.
