Glenn Robitaille's recorded discography reflects his work in the progressive, space rock, and experimental music scenes, primarily centered around his long-standing collaboration with guitarist and composer Robert Burger.

## Key Releases & Credits

* **Brainstatik – *Gizmo* (2003)**
* **Role:** Drums (acoustic / electronic)
* **Context:** Self-released debut album from the instrumental progressive/space rock project Brainstatik.


* **The Gak Omek – *Return Of The All-Powerful Light Beings* (2004)**
* **Role:** Acoustic & Electronic Drums
* **Context:** Featured as the primary live drummer on the title track (and contributing across the album) for this progressive electronic/experimental studio project led by Robert Burger.


* **Brainstatik – *The Mathematical City* (2008)**
* **Role:** Drums
* **Context:** Full-length studio release by Brainstatik under the BlueCube Music label, further expanding their mix of polyrhythmic space rock and complex time signatures.



---

> He also appeared on early regional compilations, including the 1994 cassette sampler *Neticated* (performing on the track *"Eyes Of The World"*).
