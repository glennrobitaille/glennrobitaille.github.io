Discography

A selection of studio releases and recordings featuring Glenn Robitaille on acoustic and electronic drums, highlighting work in progressive rock, space rock, and experimental music—primarily in collaboration with guitarist and composer Robert Burger.

  • Brainstatik – Gizmo (2003)
    Role: Drums (Acoustic / Electronic)
    Self-released debut album from the instrumental progressive/space rock project Brainstatik.
  • The Gak Omek – Return Of The All-Powerful Light Beings (2004)
    Role: Acoustic & Electronic Drums
    Featured as primary live drummer on the title track and contributing across the album for this progressive electronic studio project.
  • Brainstatik – The Mathematical City (2008)
    Role: Drums
    Full-length studio release on BlueCube Music, blending polyrhythmic space rock and complex time signatures.

Note: Also featured on early regional releases, including the 1994 cassette sampler Neticated (performing on the track "Eyes Of The World").
